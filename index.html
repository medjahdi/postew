Postew

A personal, self-hosted tool for scheduling and publishing content across social media.

What it does

  • Uploads videos and posts, including Shorts, Reels and TikToks, to connected accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and other social networks.
  • Publishes each post at its scheduled date and time, with its title, description, tags and privacy settings.
  • Shows the status of each scheduled post and basic statistics for published content.
Personal tool, not a public service. Postew runs on its operator's own computer and is used only to manage accounts the operator owns, or manages with their owner's permission. It is built on Postiz, open-source social media scheduling software.
