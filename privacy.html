Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

Postew ("the app") is a personal, self-hosted social media scheduling tool operated by Mohamed Medjahdi ("the operator"). It runs on the operator's own computer and is used only to publish content to accounts the operator owns, or manages with their owner's permission. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and how it can be deleted.

Data the app accesses

When an account is connected to the app, the platform (for example Google/YouTube, TikTok or Instagram) shares the following with the app, with the account owner's permission:

  • Account information: name, email address and profile picture.
  • Channel or profile information: channel or account name, ID and avatar.
  • Access tokens: the credentials the platform issues so the app can publish on the account's behalf.
  • Post data: for videos the app publishes, their IDs, links, status and basic statistics such as views, likes and comments.

The app also stores the content the operator schedules: video files, thumbnails, titles, descriptions, tags, privacy settings and publishing dates.

How the data is used

The data is used only to:

  • upload and publish the videos the account owner schedules, at the time they choose;
  • show the status of scheduled posts and basic statistics for published videos.

The data is never sold, never shared with third parties, never used for advertising or profiling, and never used to train artificial-intelligence models.

Google API Services and YouTube

Postew uses YouTube API Services to upload videos and read basic channel and video information. By connecting a YouTube account, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information received from Google is also covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Postew's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

TikTok, Instagram and other platforms

The same rules apply to every connected platform: data is used only to publish the account owner's scheduled content and show its status. Use of each platform is also subject to that platform's own terms and privacy policy.

Storage and security

All data is stored in a private database on the operator's own computer. The app is not reachable from the internet, and access tokens are only ever sent to the platform that issued them.

Retention and deletion

  • Data is kept only while an account is connected to the app.
  • Disconnecting an account in the app deletes its access tokens.
  • You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and to other platforms in their security settings.
  • To request deletion of any stored data, email mohamed@medjahdi.dev. Requests are completed within 30 days.

Cookies

This website uses no cookies and no analytics. The app's private dashboard uses a single login cookie on the operator's own computer.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
